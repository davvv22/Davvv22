01 — About

Someone who'd rather spend a Saturday debugging a broken layout than doing almost anything else.

I'm David — most people call me Davvv. I'm a 15-year-old student in Jakarta with a genuinely simple obsession: figuring out how software works, and then trying to build it myself. It started with curiosity about how websites are made, and it turned into hours of learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on my own — the kind of learning that doesn't feel like homework because I actually want to be doing it.

I'm not pretending to be an expert. I'm a beginner who takes the process seriously — reading documentation, breaking things on purpose to understand them, and slowly getting comfortable with tools like React and Git. My next big step is SMK RPL, where I'll study Software Engineering formally and push toward becoming a full stack developer who can take an idea from an empty file to a finished product.

I care about doing things properly rather than quickly. Calm, disciplined, and quietly competitive — with myself more than anyone else.
